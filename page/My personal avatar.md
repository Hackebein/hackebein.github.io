I played with an HTC Vive at a few events in 2015. I first tried VRChat at a friend's place in late 2017. I pre-ordered my own Vive Pro in April 2018 and was playing VRChat on it by the end of the month.

It took me a while to feel at home in VRChat. I'd pick a public avatar because I liked how it looked and still feel like I'd put on a costume for a show. A lot of the avatars I tried back then came from other games. They could look fine standing still, then start clipping when I moved.

## Princess and Empress

GodPrincess was the first avatar I wanted to keep. I could reach for things and move around without the avatar getting in the way. The people I played with wore that kind of avatar too, so the exaggerated proportions seemed pretty normal. GodEmpress came next, from the same family of avatars by [Godfall](https://godfall.club/collections/avatars-assets).

## Switching to Magician

I moved to GodMagician because Princess and Empress were getting harder to modify and keep up to date with VRChat. It took me more than a year to feel at home in it.

I commissioned someone to fit my Empress outfit to Magician, then uploaded the build they made to try it out. I also commissioned a completely new outfit. I never wore it. It just didn't feel like me.

## Making it my own

I spent about five years in VRChat before I started changing much beyond textures and materials. Once I was comfortable in Magician, I kept the body and started putting together outfits I wanted to wear.

Sometimes I dress up because I like how something looks on me. Sometimes an outfit gives me an idea for a character and I want to see what it feels like to be them for an evening. That's become one of my favorite parts of having an avatar I feel at home in.

I've been building the world around my avatar too, in my VTubing Studio.
